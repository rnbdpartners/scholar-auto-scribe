# LinguaForge — Adaptive Language Mastery Engine

**LinguaForge** is not another flashcard app. It is a **cognitive calibration system** that transforms passive vocabulary drills into **active neural reinforcement loops**. While conventional tools merely present material, LinguaForge observes how your brain encodes, retrieves, and forgets—then reshapes each exercise session to match your personal **memory topography**.

Born from the same spirit of automation that powers intelligent task completion, LinguaForge applies **algorithmic precision** to the art of language acquisition. Think of it as a **blacksmith for your synapses**—forging each linguistic connection with repeated, timed strikes until it becomes unbreakable steel.

## 🧠 Why Another Language Tool?

Most learners hit the **"plateau of frustration"**—the point where vocabulary stops sticking and grammar rules blur together. The root cause isn't effort; it's **inefficient repetition patterns**. Traditional spaced repetition systems (SRS) work on fixed intervals. LinguaForge works on **live cognitive feedback**.

Every time you answer, the system measures:
- **Latency** (how quickly you recall)
- **Error proximity** (how close your mistake was to the correct answer)
- **Confidence drift** (how your certainty changes across sessions)

This creates a **four-dimensional retention model**—not just *what* you know, but *how well* and *how fast* you know it.

## ⚙️ Core Architecture

### The Forge Engine (Adaptive Review Scheduler)

Unlike rigid Leitner boxes or Anki-style decks, LinguaForge uses a **fluid interval tree** that rebalances itself after every session. Miss a word three times? The algorithm compresses the review window. Nail a difficult term in under two seconds? It expands the space between reviews—but not too far, because LinguaForge understands the **curve of forgetting** is steeper than you think.

### Semantic Association Matrix

Words aren't stored in isolation. Each term is connected to:
- **Phonetic relatives** (words that sound similar)
- **Semantic neighbors** (concepts that share context)
- **Grammatical patterns** (structures where the word commonly appears)

When you struggle with one term, LinguaForge subtly introduces its **cousins** to create cross-links. This builds a **dense knowledge web** rather than isolated islands of recall.

### Pronunciation Resonance Loop

For languages with tonal or phonetic complexity, LinguaForge includes an **audio mimicry meter**. It compares your pronunciation to native speaker waves, not just for accuracy, but for **rhythm patterns**—stress, intonation, and connected speech. The system doesn't judge you; it *guides* your vocal cords toward the target shape.

## 🌍 Multilingual Mastery Without Borders

LinguaForge currently supports **34 languages** from Afro-Asiatic to Sino-Tibetan families. Each language module is independently tuned:
- **Mandarin** uses stroke-order decay tracking
- **Arabic** emphasizes root-pattern recognition
- **Japanese** balances kana/kanji dual-track memory
- **German** highlights compound word decomposition

The UI itself adapts to your target language. Learning Russian? The interface switches to Cyrillic with optional transliteration. Practicing Korean? Hangul gets priority placement in the visual hierarchy.

## 🎨 Interface: Designed for Flow State

The interface follows **minimalist friction psychology**:

- **Zero-Click Mode**: For review sessions, just type or speak—no buttons to press, no menus to navigate
- **Distraction Shield**: All non-essential elements fade when you're in active recall mode
- **Visual Progress Heatmap**: See your brain's "hot spots" where recall is strongest, and "cold zones" needing reinforcement
- **Dark/Light adaptive themes** that shift based on your system's timezone for eye comfort

## ⏱️ Session Intelligence

LinguaForge doesn't force thirty-minute blocks. It detects your **optimal study window**:
- Morning people get shorter, high-intensity drills
- Night owls receive longer, exploratory sessions
- The system signals when you're hitting diminishing returns (the **cognitive saturation point**) and suggests a rest break

### Micro-Mastery Badges

Instead of generic streaks, LinguaForge awards **skill shards**:
- 🗣️ "Echo Weaver" — 100 pronunciations within 5% accuracy
- 🧩 "Pattern Detective" — decode 50 complex grammar constructions
- ⚡ "Lightning Recall" — 20 consecutive answers under 1.5 seconds

These shards unlock **special review modes**—like "Pressure Forge" (timed rapid-fire) or "Deep Anvil" (multi-step sentence building).

## 🌐 Community Cerebral Sync

The dashboard displays:

These aren't vanity metrics. They're actionable diagnostics that shape your study plan.

## 🔧 Technical Implementation

Built with:
- React 19 for the frontend
- WebAssembly core for high-speed interval calculations
